# RNG Esports Website

Official website for Renegade Esports at Ohlone College.

Live Website: https://rngesports.com/

## About

RNG Esports is the official esports club at Ohlone College working to give opportunities for community college students in the field of esports. This website serves to represent this community by showcasing the weekly events, sponsorships, esport classes, and esport teams. 
This websites was built to be scaleable to allow easy additions of events, teams, and partnerships. Renegade Esports supports 800+ community members, hosted over 40+ in-person events, and 50+ online events in the past year. 
RNG Esports is supported by Central Computers, ASUS, BisectHosting, and First Dawn.
